# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Criticise ideas and code, not people, and assume good faith. Respect privacy and differing experiences. Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, sexual attention and disclosure of private information are not accepted here.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place the project is talked about.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation or block accounts. The response reflects how serious the conduct was and whether it was repeated.

To report a problem, use the repository's private "Report a vulnerability" form (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and mark it as a conduct report. Do not post private reports in public issues.
